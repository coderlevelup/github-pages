## What It Is

**SAPIENT** — South African Planetary Institute for Exploring Nature with Technology

A collaborative initiative co-founded by David Campey (CoderLevelUp / Afrolabs) and Dr. Matthew S. Huber (Planetary Science Institute / University of KwaZulu-Natal).

SAPIENT brings together planetary science and technology education — using the excitement of space exploration and robotics to engage learners, educators, and the public.

Website: https://sapient.rocks (currently a Google Sites page)

### The People

**David Campey** — software developer, tech entrepreneur, CoderLevelUp founder, Afrolabs founder. Founding member of SAPIENT.

**Dr. Matthew S. Huber** — Research Scientist at the Planetary Science Institute (Tucson, AZ), Senior Lecturer at UKZN. Expert in impact craters and planetary science. Work featured in Newsweek, Washington Post, Fox News, EOS, Live Science. Has run NASA workshops, organised European Astrobiology Institute conferences. Founding member of SAPIENT.

## Activities

- **Mars Yard** — a simulated Martian landscape for running rover code (see `mars-yard.md`)
- **Iziko Planetarium lectures** — public science talks on planetary science and technology topics, convened at the Iziko Planetarium, Cape Town
- **Turtle-bots** — making robotics more accessible and exciting; the turtle-bots project connects Python turtle programming to physical robots
