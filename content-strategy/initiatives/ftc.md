# Initiative: FTC (First Tech Challenge)

## Tags

## What It Is

The First Tech Challenge (FTC) is a FIRST robotics competition for teams of young people aged 12–18. Teams design, build, programme, and operate robots to compete in an alliance format. Beyond the robot, teams are judged on community outreach, engineering documentation, and gracious professionalism.

CoderLevelUp supports FTC in the Western Cape through **IGNITE Robotics International**, the South African organisation coordinating FTC teams.

## Angle for the Site

- Primary angle: **volunteering** — FTC needs mentors, judges, referees, field reset volunteers
- Secondary angle: following the teams / celebrating achievements
- Tertiary angle: sponsorship (Texpand and Autonova are fundraising for Houston)
- Audience: local tech professionals, educators, parents, potential sponsors

### The Volunteering Hook

FTC events only happen because of volunteers. CoderLevelUp's role in the FIRST ecosystem is partly about growing the volunteer base — people with engineering, coding, or education backgrounds who can mentor teams or serve at events.
