# Initiative: LoCO AUV

## Tags
`loco-auv`

## What It Is

A community collaboration to build and run the **LoCO AUV** (Low Cost Open-Source Autonomous Underwater Vehicle) in South Africa.

> "Communities build robots and robots build communities."

The LoCO AUV is an open-source underwater vehicle designed at the University of Minnesota to be affordable and accessible, encouraging creative learning through experiencing technology.

**Why:** Capture the imagination of learners, industry, and the community — catalyse and inspire exploration of new frontiers with technology, specifically the ocean economy and marine robotics.

## The Learning Pathway

LoCO AUV integrates into a scaffolded learning path for school and university learners:

**Level 1:** Scratch project — move a sprite underwater  
**Level 2:** Micro:bit robotics — 2-wheeled land bot in sim and real  
**Level 3:** Abstracted LoCO Python (turtle-style commands), sim & real  
**Level N:** Native ROS LoCO pathway — ROS turtle tutorial → Gazebo basics → LoCO ROS Gazebo sim → to real
